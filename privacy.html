Privacy Policy

HexBeat: Boxing Rhythm for Apple Vision Pro · effective October 26, 2026

HexBeat has no account of its own, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We don't run servers and don't receive your data. The online features use Apple services, and only one of them publishes anything about you: the shared leaderboards for your own songs, and only if you turn them on.

What stays on your Apple Vision Pro

Your settings, calibration, scores and records, and the songs you add with their beat maps are stored only inside the app on your device. They are included in your device backup like any other app data and are deleted when you delete the app.

Your hands and your space

HexBeat uses hand tracking to see your punches and world sensing to place the hexagon in front of you. This information is processed on your device as you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Music you add

The audio files you add stay on your device; they are never uploaded or shared. To recognize a song, HexBeat makes an audio signature (a short digital fingerprint of about 12 seconds of the song, which can't be turned back into sound) and sends it to Apple's Shazam service, which returns the title, artist and a link to the cover art. The cover image is then loaded from Apple's servers. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to these requests.

Apple Health (optional)

Only if you allow it, HexBeat saves each song you play as a boxing workout with estimated active calories, and reads your weight to make that estimate (it uses 70 kg when no weight is available). Health data stays in the Health app: we never share it and never use it for advertising or marketing. You can turn this off at any time in HexBeat's Settings (Save Workouts to Apple Health) and change access in the Settings app.

Game Center (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores on the HexBeat Originals are submitted to Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center nickname, where other players can see them. Game Center is run by Apple under your Apple Account settings.

Shared leaderboards for your songs (off unless you turn them on)

If you choose Share My Library Scores, then for each song that Shazam identified, HexBeat stores the following in its public iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), where other HexBeat players can see it:

  • your Game Center nickname;
  • your best score, accuracy and level on that song;
  • a coded player identifier derived from your Game Center ID, used only to keep one entry per player and never shown.

The song itself is stored only as a coded key, never its title, artist or audio. Turning off Share My Library Scores in HexBeat's Settings deletes these entries for the songs in your library. To delete entries for songs you have already removed, email us.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up of our own.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics of our own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

HexBeat has no ads and no tracking. The only information other people can see is your Game Center nickname with your scores, through Game Center and, if turned on, the shared leaderboards. Parents can manage Game Center in Screen Time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.

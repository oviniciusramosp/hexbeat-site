HexBeat: Boxing Rhythm for Apple Vision Pro

Getting started

How much space do I need?

About 1 meter (3 feet) all around you. Stretch your arms out and turn around: nothing should be in reach, not furniture, walls, pets or people. Warm up with a few slow punches before your first song.

How do I place the hexagon?

Before each song, move the hexagon with the handle under it, or tap Reposition. In Settings, under Body & Placement, set the distance, height and size; turn on Preview in Room to see the hexagon move while you adjust. The pads should sit where your fist lands with your elbow still a little bent.

How do I pause?

Punch or tap the pause button to the right of the hexagon. It also works before the song starts, to go back to your songs.

Timing and scoring

My punches feel early or late

In Settings, under Timing, tap Calibrate and follow the beat for a few seconds. HexBeat measures your timing and sets the latency offset for you. Keep your hands in view and play in good light.

How is a punch scored?

Each hit earns points for timing, power and how close to the center of the pad you land. Hits in a row raise your multiplier up to 8x; a miss halves it. With No Fail on, you keep playing when your energy runs out, but notes after that count half.

Your music

How do I add my own songs?

In My Library, tap Add Music and choose audio files you own. HexBeat maps every beat into punches on five levels. Songs bought on the iTunes Store work; in Settings, Help explains how to bring them from your Mac. Apple Music downloads and older protected (.m4p) purchases can't be added.

Why wasn't my song identified?

Recognition needs an internet connection, and some songs aren't in Shazam's catalog. Tap Identify Song to try again, or Edit Details to type the title and artist yourself.

The punches don't follow the beat of my song

Open the song in My Library and use Check Beat to shift the timing or fix the tempo.

Workout and leaderboards

How are calories estimated?

From how many punches you throw per minute, your weight from the Health app (70 kg when there is none) and your active time, without pauses. It's an estimate, not a medical measurement. Turn on Save Workouts to Apple Health in Settings to keep each song as a boxing workout, or tap Start Workout to save several songs as one session until you tap End Workout.

How do leaderboards work?

The HexBeat Originals have a Game Center leaderboard for every song and level: sign in to Game Center to take part. Songs you add are compared with players who have the same recording once Shazam has identified them, under a random name unless you choose to show your nickname (Settings › Show My Name on Leaderboards). Those boards rank by accuracy, because the punches depend on each player's audio file.

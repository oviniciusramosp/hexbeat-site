HexBeat

Punch to the beat on Apple Vision Pro.

Hand tracking · Five levels · Game Center

Punch to the beat

Pulses of light race from the center of a hexagon to six pads around you. Hit each pad the moment the light arrives: every punch is scored on timing, power and accuracy.

Chain hits. Go gold.

Every combo raises your multiplier. Reach 8x and the arena turns gold in Fever mode, with your hands on fire.

Climb the leaderboards

Five levels on every song, from Easy to Expert+, each with its own Game Center leaderboard. Songs you add can be compared with players who have the same recording, ranked by accuracy.

Every punch, scored

After each song, see your rank, best combo, timing, power and accuracy on every pad, and the calories you burned.

Your music

Add audio files you own. HexBeat maps every beat into punches on five levels and recognizes the song to show its cover and artist.

A real workout

Watch your calories live in the arena and save a whole session, or each song, as a boxing workout in the Health app.

Fits your space

Set the distance, height and size of the hexagon. Play in your room or inside the HexBeat arena. No controllers needed.

What you need

  • Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 27 or later
  • About 1 meter (3 feet) of clear space around you
  • Internet only for leaderboards and song recognition
